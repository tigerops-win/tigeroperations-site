How we work

///

Machines are customers

Agents don't fill out contact forms. They read documentation, check a price, and pay — in one motion. Everything we ship is built to be bought that way: documented, priced, and callable without a human in the loop.

///

Pay for output, not access

No seats, no tiers, no monthly minimums. A lookup costs cents when it's made and nothing when it isn't. Pricing follows the unit of value, so our revenue only exists when a customer got something worth paying for.

///

Precision over scale

Small tools that do one job exactly right. Every endpoint and every repaired file is checked against a stated standard before it ships — the business is built on being correct, not on being loud.
